Deep Glow Privacy Policy

App: Deep Glow (Android, package com.tinytapkids.deepglow)

Developer: Tiny Tap Kids Studio

Website: https://hirena85.github.io

Contact: hrr4.h.a@gmail.com

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Short version: Deep Glow is a free game for a general audience (13+) that shows ads from Google AdMob and offers optional online leaderboards through Google Play Games. We (Tiny Tap Kids Studio) do not run any servers and do not collect, store or sell your personal information ourselves. Your game progress stays on your device, apart from scores sent to the leaderboards if you use Google Play Games. Google processes some data as described below.

1. Who we are

Deep Glow ("the app", "the game") is made by Tiny Tap Kids Studio ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at hrr4.h.a@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your device, in the app’s local storage: your best depth and top 10 dives, your best Daily Dive, pearls, unlocked creatures and upgrades, your selected creature, daily mission progress, the number of dives you have played and whether you have seen the How to Play guide. This data is never sent to us or to any server we operate. If you use Google Play Games, your scores are also sent to Google, as described in section 5.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Android's own device backup may include this data in your Google account backup if you have device backup turned on. That backup is controlled by you and Google.

3. No accounts of our own

The game has no user accounts of its own, no chat and no user-generated content. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional and is handled by Google. Everything except the online leaderboards works without it. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information. The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows full-screen ads from Google AdMob between some dives, never during a dive. It also offers optional rewarded ads, which you can choose to watch to continue a dive or double your pearls. Ads are limited to a "PG" content rating. To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location is derived
  • information about the device and app, such as model, operating system version, app version, and launch and performance diagnostics
  • your interactions with ads, such as views and taps

This data is encrypted in transit. Google uses it for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention as described in its policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

5. Online leaderboards (Google Play Games)

The game offers two online leaderboards, Deepest Dive and Daily Dive, through Google Play Games Services. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, which can happen automatically on your device, the game sends Google your best depth and your Daily Dive depths. If you are not signed in, the game sends no scores. Google Play Games Services handles:

  • your Play Games identity, such as your gamertag and avatar, which is shared with the game when you sign in
  • your leaderboard scores
  • analytics and diagnostics that Google collects to keep its service stable and improve it

This data is encrypted in transit and handled under the Google Privacy Policy linked above. Other players may see your gamertag, avatar and scores, depending on the visibility you choose in your Play Games profile. The game does not use your friends list or Google's Saved Games feature, and we do not copy this data to any server of ours.

You can change your visibility or delete your Play Games data from your Play Games profile or your Google Account.

6. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent or show your privacy choices before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy" button on the game's creature screen, which is shown where this option is required. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads.

7. Children

The game is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data retention and your rights

We do not hold any of your data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to retain, export or delete. Data processed by Google for ads and Play Games is handled under Google's policies linked above, and you can manage it through your Google account, your Play Games profile and your device settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools, and contact us with any questions.

9. Security

All communication between the app and Google's ad, consent and Play Games services uses encryption (HTTPS/TLS). The game does not transmit your data to us, or to any party other than Google.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example because a new feature needs it, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
